About

I'm a senior structural and bridge engineer with 13+ years designing, independently checking, and now leading the on-site technical delivery of complex bridge and metro infrastructure — currently the site technical lead on the Wadi Laban Cable-Stayed Bridge in Riyadh, and rail projects across the UAE, Jordan, Saudi Arabia and Oman.

My work sits at the harder end of bridge engineering: balanced-cantilever, extradosed and cable-stayed crossings, and segmental box-girder viaducts, for metro and rail authorities in India, Saudi Arabia, the UK and Australia. I move fluently between Indian, AASHTO/ACI, British, Euro and Saudi codes, and between detailed design, independent proof-checking, and the site — where I'm now the daily interface between PMC, Designer and subcontractors on lifting, temporary PT, geometry control and NCR/FCR resolution.

Alongside the calculations, I build the design tools and mentor the engineers that let a small team deliver large infrastructure — and I still go to site, because a drawing is only correct until it meets the ground.
